It was a stormy evening, the wind howling through the rocky shores as the moon hung low in the night sky like a silver snake. The keeper, Jack, stood at the base of the lighthouse, his hair white as snow, his eyes sunken to the whites. He was alone in the lair of his own domain, his home protected behind the creaky doors of the old structure.

Jack heard the last beat of the sea drumming against the rocks and he set off to find the source of the strange message drifting on the waves. He had been on duty alone, the fog burning behind him like a shadow. He had to find it. He crept down rocky cliffs, the faint sound of his footsteps making him pause at the old lighthouse. It was like not being there, like there was a mirror staring at him from the darkness.

As he strolled down the rocky path, he saw nothing out of the ordinary: no birds, no trees, no nothing of his home world. Just a hollow, rocky form that seemed to be watching from the water far below.

Suddenly, a small wave crashed over his head, revealing a flaking slate of a shell. Jack, he knew the shape of the shell. It was shaped like a miniature shell; no larger than a snail. In this shell, he saw the words: 'Hidden Island'.

Jack opened the shell and his eyes were met with a sight that made his jaw hang open. The small world was illuminated by the faint glow of tiny candles dancing. Jack, he knew what the message was. It was a symbol for the lighthouse keepers of the hidden islands, the islands where the sea's mystery still lingers.
